# Task 9 — Interpretation Report

Run `python analysis.py` to generate the full report from the downloaded Titanic dataset.

## Questions answered
1. What do mean, median, and mode tell us about numeric columns?
2. How spread out are values, as measured by standard deviation?
3. What do the 25th and 75th percentiles tell us about the middle 50% of observations?
4. How do survival rates vary by passenger class?

The script calculates these statistics directly from the selected dataset, exports them to Excel, and replaces this starter report with data-driven findings.
